Encrypting and decrypting using =gpg=.

* Encrypt

  You can encrypt with a passphrase file without having any kind of saved keys
  or anything. Just do this:

  #+begin_src sh
    cat "Hello world.\n" > foo.txt
    gpg --armour --symmetric foo.txt
  #+end_src
